**Card** — editorial image card: framed 4:3 photo, category tag, serif title, body, uppercase read-more link.

```jsx
<Card image="/assets/photos/beach-yoga.webp" tag="Wellness" tagTone="teal"
  title="Move at the water's edge" href="#" linkLabel="Read the guide">
  Sunrise flows on the sand before the workday begins.
</Card>
```

Use in 3-up service/blog grids. Photo zooms slowly on hover.
