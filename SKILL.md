If the user invokes this skill without any other guidance, ask them what they want to build or design, ask some questions, and act as an expert designer who outputs HTML artifacts _or_ production code, depending on the need.

Quick orientation:
- **Voice:** warm, direct, honest — never corporate or "AI". Sentence case; short lines; real typographic marks; no emoji. CTAs are "Request an Invitation" / "RSVP".
- **Color:** **neutral-led** — warm mushroom off-white + muted near-black do the work; photos and type carry the color. Accents (Ares Red, Smoky Blue, dusty light blue, Lemon) are used **rarely**. On photos, text and buttons are muted black or white.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display/headers), Cormorant Garamond (italic ledes/quotes), Jost (body + letterspaced labels), Yeseva One (funky display serif — wordmark & big accents). All Google Fonts substitutes — swap if real files arrive.
- **Feel:** editorial travel magazine, oversized serif headlines, warm faded photography in boxy hairline frames, thin rule dividers, pill buttons, gentle unhurried motion.
- **Tokens:** link `styles.css`. Components live under `window.TheFoundersEditDesignSystem_0cfd4a` after loading `_ds_bundle.js`.
- **Full example:** `ui_kits/website/` is the real invitation landing page — the best reference for composition.
